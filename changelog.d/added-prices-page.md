- A Prices page (`#/prices`), open to visitors: every tool with what it is
  for and its live fare, and how long a top-up lasts. It is the fleet's
  `PriceSheet` in the almanac's type, with the fares in honey. Hovering a tool, or tapping it on a phone,
  shows how to call it. It needs `@tollbooth-dpyc/web` 2.3.0.
