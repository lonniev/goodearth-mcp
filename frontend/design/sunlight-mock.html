This scene is illustrative: the trees and hills are drawn for the mock, and the shadows are computed from them with real sun positions for latitude 44°N. On a real plot, Good Earth takes tree heights from a 1 m global canopy-height map, terrain from a 30 m elevation model, and sunshine from ten years of hourly records for the plot's own weather cell. Solar figures are a screening estimate, scaled here to 1,240 kWh per kW for open sky.
